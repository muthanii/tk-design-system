Photos show real people and real work: no stock images, no AI renders. On a photo band, lay the `primary-overlay` wash over the image and set the text in white. Don't put `ink` text straight onto a photo.

These are 1280px wide, fine for cards and bands up to about 1280px. A full-width hero needs larger originals, which the project's media team will have.
