# Logos

- `teebat_karbala_company_logo.png`: the Teebat Karbala company logo from the website header. An eight-point star of `logo-blue` (#105989) corner marks around `logo-aqua` (#78c8d9) diamonds and a scalloped centre, with طيبة كربلاء above "Teebat Karbala". 4000×1139 PNG with a transparent background. This is the primary logo.
- `kia_logo_large.jpg`: the Karbala International Airport lockup at 2048×1152, from the project's Facebook cover. It sits on a light grey patterned ground, so crop to the logo before use, or use it only where that ground fits.
- `kia_logo_reversed.jpg`: the airport lockup reversed, white and gold on navy, 2048×2045 (the Facebook profile image). Use it on dark grounds, social avatars and the blue footer.
- `kia_logo.jpg`: the original 200×200 airport logo on white. Superseded by `kia_logo_large.jpg` for anything larger than 200px.

Place the full-colour logos on white or `surface`, with clear space at least the height of the star's corner mark (at least `space-30`). Use the reversed airport logo on dark grounds. Keep the company logo at least 120px wide on screen. Never recolour, stretch, crop into or redraw a logo, never set the star without its wordmark, and never place the full-colour logos on photos or on `primary`.
