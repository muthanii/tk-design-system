# Header

The site header: a white top bar (`topbar` style, 14px `ink` links, `line` bottom border, `space-15` padding) over the main nav.

Nav links are Cairo 18px weight 500 in `ink`, padded `space-25` × 12px. The current page and hovered links turn `primary` with a 3px `primary` underline rounded on top (`radius-5`). The Arabic toggle sits at the far end as a small primary button.

The company logo (`assets/Logos/teebat_karbala_company_logo.png`) sits at the left, about 48px tall. The consumer provides the menu items. Mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`.
