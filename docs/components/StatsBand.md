# StatsBand

The "Facts and Figures" counter band (`.fun-facts`): project photography under a `primary-overlay` (primary at 80%), with white stats.

Each stat has a 70px round icon outlined in 2px white, a `stat` number and a `small` caption. On hover the icon fills white and turns `primary`. The consumer provides the background photo (set it on the band behind the overlay), the IcoFont icons (28px, as on the site: `cement-mix`, `vehicle-dozer`, `building-alt`, `location-pin`) and the figures. The preview uses `site-steel-frame.jpg` from the Photography group.
