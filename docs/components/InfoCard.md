# InfoCard

The navy card row under the hero (`.single-schedule`): `navy` fill, `radius-5`, `space-30` padding, white `card-title` heading and white body text.

On hover it lifts 5px with a soft `primary` glow (0.3s ease-out). Use three in a row, each a short title and one or two sentences. The site also places a large faint white icon in the corner at 20% opacity; that is optional.
