# Footer

The site footer: a `primary` band with a 10% black shade on the top part (`.footer-top`, `space-100` padding on the site), three columns (Contact Us, Quick Links, Important Links) with white `card-title` headings, then a centred copyright line on plain `primary`.

Social links are 34px white-outlined circles holding 16px IcoFont brand icons (`facebook`, `twitter`, `instagram`, `youtube-play`), that fill white on hover. The consumer provides the columns and links.
